InvestRight – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

InvestRight is a financial education and planning app by Tarakkii Wealth Management, Vadodara.

1. No account needed

You can use InvestRight without signing up. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or PAN.

2. Risk profile answers

When you use the Risk Profiler, your answers are sent securely to our server only to calculate your result. They are not stored.

3. Calculator inputs

Amounts and years you enter in the SIP, SWP, Lump Sum and Goal planners are calculated on your device and are not sent to us.

4. No ads or tracking

We do not show ads, sell data, or use tracking or advertising tools.

5. Services we use

The app is hosted on Render and uses Google Fonts for text styling. These services may see basic technical details such as your IP address, as any website does.

6. Security

All connections use HTTPS encryption.

7. Age

InvestRight is meant for users aged 18 and above.
